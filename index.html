Credits & Acknowledgements

AmritSetu is built on the shoulders of giants. We gratefully acknowledge these extraordinary sources that make authentic scripture access possible for everyone.

📖 Vedabase
The complete Bhagavad Gita and Srimad Bhagavatam with original Sanskrit, word-by-word transliteration, and Prabhupada's commentary. Verify every verse against the original.
Visit vedabase.io ↗
📚 Sacred Texts Archive
Since 1999, sacred-texts.com has preserved ancient scriptures from every tradition freely for all of humanity. An invaluable scholarly resource for Vedas, Upanishads, Manusmriti and more.
Visit sacred-texts.com ↗
📖 Wisdomlib
Wisdomlib.org provides verse-by-verse scholarly translations with Sanskrit, transliteration and commentary for all major Puranas and Hindu texts. An outstanding free resource for serious students.
Visit wisdomlib.org ↗
🕉️ Vedabase — Bhaktivedanta Book Trust
Srila Prabhupada's translations of the Bhagavad Gita As It Is and Srimad Bhagavatam — among the most widely read scripture translations in the world. Published by the Bhaktivedanta Book Trust.
Visit vedabase.io ↗
🏛️ Gita Press, Gorakhpur
Since 1923, Gita Press has published authentic Sanskrit editions of all major Hindu scriptures at the lowest possible cost for the benefit of all. Their Shiva Purana and Padma Purana editions are in our library.
Visit gitapress.org ↗
🌟 AWGP — Pt. Shriram Sharma Acharya
The Akhand Jyoti Sansthan and AWGP preserve Pt. Shriram Sharma Acharya's complete works including his landmark Hindi translations of all 108 Upanishads — the backbone of our Upanishad library.
Visit awgp.org ↗
🏹 Valmiki Ramayana
Valmikiramayan.net provides the complete original Sanskrit Valmiki Ramayana with transliteration and English translation — making the world's oldest epic accessible to all.
Visit valmikiramayan.net ↗
🌐 Internet Archive
Archive.org has preserved thousands of out-of-print and rare scripture editions freely for the world. Many of the scholarly translations in our library were sourced from this invaluable digital archive.
